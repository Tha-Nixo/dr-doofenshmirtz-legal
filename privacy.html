Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Dr. Doofenshmirtz handles information when users interact with the bot through Discord.

2. Information Processed

When the bot is used, it may process information provided by Discord as necessary to perform its functions, including:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server ID
  • Discord channel ID
  • Interaction and command data
  • Content entered into publication forms

3. Purpose of Processing

This information is processed only to execute commands, validate interactions, generate previews, and publish messages or embeds requested by authorized users.

4. Data Storage

Dr. Doofenshmirtz does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of user-submitted publication data. Information entered during the publication process is temporarily held in memory while the interaction is being completed.

5. Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold, rented, or intentionally shared with third parties by the bot operator.

6. Discord and Hosting Providers

Use of the bot also involves Discord's own infrastructure and policies. Discord may process information according to its own Privacy Policy. The legal pages for this bot are hosted using GitHub Pages, which is operated by GitHub.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit access to the bot and its infrastructure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's functionality, data processing, or hosting arrangements change.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the bot administrator through the Discord server where the bot is installed.
